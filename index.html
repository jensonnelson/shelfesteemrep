About Shelf Esteem

You deserve a home that supports you.
We'll help you find your way there.

There's a reason the company is called Shelf Esteem. The way you feel about your home shapes how you feel about yourself, and vice versa. When your space is chaotic, it's harder to think clearly, rest deeply, or show up as the person you want to be.

Shelf Esteem was founded on a simple belief: organizing isn't about perfection. It's about designing a life where things have a home, routines make sense, and getting back to order takes minutes, not hours.

Every client comes with a different history, a different floor plan, and a different definition of "done." We meet you there. Whether you need a guiding voice, an extra pair of hands, or someone who understands that ADHD and clutter have a complicated relationship. We're here, without judgment, with a plan.

What We Offer

Services built
around how you live.

01
Walk & Talk
$200 · One-time session

Ready to tackle your own decluttering but want a savvy sounding board? In one focused hour, a professional organizer tours your space, listens deeply, and delivers tailored strategies for every room, plus a curated product list to help you move forward with confidence.

02
In-Home Organizing
$90 / hour

More than tidying, we analyze how you live. We assess what you own, how your rooms are laid out, and how you use them. Then we design storage and workflow systems that fit your routine, so the order we create lasts through your busiest days.

03
KonMari Wardrobe
$90 / hour

When your wardrobe no longer reflects who you are, decluttering can feel daunting. Using the KonMari method, we evaluate each piece against your current lifestyle and body, keeping what fits and sparks joy, releasing the rest with intention.

04
To-Do Help
$90 / hour

That list of half-finished tasks? Consider it handled. We assemble furniture, hang art, organize the garage, sort the kids' clothes, whatever has been living in your head rent-free. You'll close the day with a home that feels complete.

05
ADHD & Mood Support
$90 / hour

If you start in one room only to find yourself in another, you're not alone. We break organizing into manageable steps, teach focus strategies, and build systems rooted in empathy, with practical habits that help you maintain calm through the chaos.
